# Retina AI on the public dataset "APTOS 2019 train"

**Research use only.** The model is not a medical device; these figures do not make it fit for clinical use.

## Data
- 3662 images from D:\datasets\APTOS2019\train_images; labels D:\datasets\APTOS2019\train.csv (columns "id_code", "diagnosis").
- Public dataset, not Sampada patients: shows how the model does on another camera and population, not on the Remidio FOP.
- Each image is resampled on its own (which images share a patient is not known), so intervals may be too narrow.

## Model
- Arko007/diabetic-retinopathy-v1, commit 05e5b0f, weights SHA-256 cd4b10c3e5bb1043ac01098284a1136bb423a0eafe7a2445587e969d370f3ffc
- grader 1.0.0, output native_probabilities

## Cautions
- Training overlap: the model card names IDRiD as its only training data; nothing published says APTOS was used, but this cannot be verified (no training code or image list).

## Reproducibility
- 95% intervals: percentile bootstrap, 2000 resamples of single images, seed 20261025.
- Run 2026-10-25T12:40:42.408Z with node dist/cli.js retina-eval-external.
- Checked 25 Oct 2026 by an independent recount in Python from the per-image CSV: confusion matrix, TP 1047 / FN 440 / FP 802 / TN 1373, sensitivity 70.41%, specificity 63.13%, weighted kappa 0.437 all agree; 3,662 of 3,662 labels match train.csv. AUROC recounts as 0.702 from the CSV's 6-decimal scores (ties among saturated scores) against 0.703 from full-precision scores; CSVs now keep full precision.
